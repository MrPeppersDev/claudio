1. Governance queue card order will change. Within any given priority tier (all the red cards as a group, all the amber cards as a group), cards will now order by age (older floats up) and dependent count (higher breaks age ties). Across tiers nothing changes — Crisis/Urgent still sit above Actionable still sits above Advisory/Background.
2. The "Blast radius: N dependent entries" section will appear/disappear for different cards. Before, it showed whenever the Governor scalar was nonzero — which meant some cards showed "Blast radius: 0 dependent entries" (nonsense) and others hid the section even when real dependents existed. Now the section appears if-and-only-if there are actual downstream dependents, and the number shown is the real count.
